## The split's CPU at a release (scaled from the bench by bz2 bytes)

- split CPU: 11.8 core-h (2h57m on 4 cores); the bz2 decode alone 54% of it: 6.3 core-h (1h35m)
- a hashing-only pass (decode + lines + id + xxh3): 6.7 core-h, 1h40m on 4 cores, against the dump's download 1h59m
- the rest (reshape, build, encode, writes): 5.4 core-h; at the measured churn 3 core-min of it
