# The workstation bench, and a merge rule for it (proposal, pending the maintainer's approval)

**Status:** the recipe is built (`just bench-i9`, `just submit`; bench/README.md, "The
workstation bench"). The rule change below is **pending the maintainer's approval**. It is
not in CLAUDE.md, and nothing merges on it until he approves it.

## Why

CI's bench runs on 4-core GitHub runners. A change whose gain needs more cores can't show
there. The latest case is the mibz2 0.2.5 bump (lmmx/wikidata-pq#219): 0.2.4's MTF
registers, and 0.2.5's three teams of three, which mibz2 plans only at 10 or more threads on
a 10-core, 20 MiB-L3 machine. On the runners, parity passed and no end-to-end gain showed, as
expected. The PR merged on the maintainer's decision, outside the rule that a merge needs an
end-to-end figure that improves beyond noise.

The maintainer approved a release-shaped bench on his i9-10900K (10 cores, 20 threads,
20 MiB L3). It is opt-in per pull request (the label `bench:i9`) and submitted automatically.
There is no manual upload, and the run is identified from its bundle, not typed in.

## What was built

- **`just bench-i9 PR [BASE]`** (`bench/i9.sh`): the Benchmark workflow's pull-request run,
  reproduced on the workstation:
  - the PR tier, from `tiers.py`, with no labels and an empty body;
  - 3 rounds, each a forward and a reverse job, in sequence;
  - before: HEAD's merge base with origin/master, or BASE. After: HEAD, committed and pushed;
  - each arm in its own worktree, on its own lockfile;
  - the data at the workflow's `DATA_COMMIT`, checked;
  - the extras after each job's core;
  - `combine.py` over the six results, as the finish job runs it.

  One new directory per attempt, bundled on exit with `outcome.txt` (`COMPLETE`,
  `PARITY FAILED` or `INCOMPLETE: <step>`) and `meta.txt` (the PR, both commits, `nproc`,
  `lscpu`).
- **`just submit bench-i9 PR`** (`bench/submit.sh`, ported from lmmx/mibz2): it archives the
  bundle on the orphan `evidence` branch, then posts its identity, SHA-256 and summary on
  the pull request, once.

**What it does not change:** the pipeline's defaults. The native decoder stays capped at 4
threads (`dump.bz2_threads`). So the i9 bench shows mibz2 0.2.5's teams only for a change
that raises the cap or sets the threads in code. On the default cap, the i9 measures the
0.2.4 MTF registers and whatever else scales with cores (lbzip2, Polars' pool).

## The proposed rule

In CLAUDE.md, under "Measuring", the list "A change merges when:" now reads:

> - **A change merges when:**
>   - parity passes;
>   - CI is green;
>   - an end-to-end figure (release ETA, pipeline or finalise ETA, CPU, wall, peak RSS,
>     scratch disk) improves beyond noise (its 95% interval excludes zero);
>   - no other figure regresses unacceptably.

The proposal changes its third item, and adds one item after the list:

> - **A change merges when:**
>   - parity passes;
>   - CI is green;
>   - an end-to-end figure (release ETA, pipeline or finalise ETA, CPU, wall, peak RSS,
>     scratch disk) improves beyond noise (its 95% interval excludes zero), on CI's bench
>     or, for a PR labelled `bench:i9`, on the workstation bench;
>   - no other figure regresses unacceptably.
> - **The workstation bench** (`bench:i9`, bench/README.md): for a change whose effect needs
>   more cores than CI's runners have. The maintainer runs `just submit bench-i9 <pr>` on the
>   PR's benched head. The figure that meets the gain condition is the submitted run's: its
>   outcome `COMPLETE`, its after arm the head that merges, and its 95% interval excluding
>   zero. CI must still show parity passing and no other figure regressing unacceptably. The
>   i9 run adds to CI's checks and replaces none.

## Open points for the maintainer

- **Which figures count from the i9:** the proposal allows any end-to-end figure, as on CI.
  A narrower rule would allow only the figures the PR names as core-bound, such as the
  pipeline ETA or wall.
- **The head:** the proposal requires the i9 run's after arm to be the merging head. CLAUDE.md
  allows a later head if its commits change nothing the bench measures; the same allowance
  could apply here.
- **The harness version:** 17.17.0, a minor bump. Earlier workstation-only tools (bench/full,
  #200; bz2threads, #210) shipped without one, so the coordinator may drop the bump if that
  is the convention.
